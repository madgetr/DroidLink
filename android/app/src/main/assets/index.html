Radar

What the droid has seen, around it (it faces up). Faint = an old or unsure memory: people fade in about a minute, pets in a few, furniture in half an hour, and everything fades as the droid drives (its sense of position drifts). Dashed lines are directions with no distance. The faint path is where it has been. The green squares are floor it knows it can drive on, red squares are where it got blocked, and a dashed blue line is the route it's taking. Zoom with + / −, the mouse wheel, or pinch; Auto fits everything.

Talk

"Tell" goes to the agent like talking to it by name (it may start a mission). "Say it out loud" makes the droid speak your words in its voice.
While you have control, the agent pauses. Driving takes control automatically.
Keys: arrows / WASD drive, Q / E head, Space stops.
